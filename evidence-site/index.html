When does AI-driven recovery add value over deterministic rules?

Correct outcomes are not the same as full recoveries: a correct outcome can be a verified recovery, a correct escalation, a partial recovery with escalation, or correctly doing nothing.

Important limitations

  • Same authors. Prospective scenarios were written by the team that developed the agent, after inspecting this simulator. They are not independently designed real-world unseen failures.
  • Small, correlated samples.
  • No claim of statistical significance.
  • Simulation. The backend, faults and hidden checks are hand-built. Results do not transfer to arbitrary production systems.
  • Detection gap. Both systems share one detector, which misses backlogs that grow intermittently.

How it was tested

  • Same scenarios, starting states, tools, safety controls and independent verification for both systems.
  • Scenarios were defined after the production version was frozen. Each registered scenario and seed ran once, with no reruns, and hidden checks that neither engine can see scored the result.
  • An internally built, synthetic e-commerce environment, with no real customer or transaction records. The language models were used only for inference and tool calling. They were not trained or fine-tuned on this data.
  • Tool results were stored cut to characters, so some recorded results are incomplete.
